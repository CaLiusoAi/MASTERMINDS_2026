  A2. IDENTITY

      distinguishes them.
      Formal: If s1 is not equal to s2, then there exists a predicate P
      such that P(s1) is not equal to P(s2).

  A4. NON-CONTRADICTION
      A proposition cannot be simultaneously true and false in the
      same respect.
      Formal: Not(p AND not-p).

  A5. DETERMINACY

      single result.

   P_i = [m*_i(0), m*_i(1), m*_i(2), ..., m*_i(9999)]

   The canonical path for dimension i is the ordered sequence of all 10,000

   dimension's state space.

   S_global(t) = {S_1(t), S_2(t), S_3(t), ..., S_37(t)}

   The global state at time t is the union of all 37 dimensional states
   at that time.

6. GLOBAL CANONICAL PATH

   P = [P_1, P_2, P_3, ..., P_37]

   The complete system path is the collection of all 37 dimensional paths.
