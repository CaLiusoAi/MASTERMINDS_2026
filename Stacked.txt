Complete system axioms — fully expanded, irreducible, and internally ordered.

This is the entire governing stack, from minimal logical substrate to applied execution law.

⸻

I. Ontological Base
	1.	Existence
Something is.
	2.	Identity
What is, is itself.
	3.	Distinction
If two states are not identical, they are distinguishable.
	4.	Non-Contradiction
A proposition cannot be true and false in the same respect.
	5.	Determinacy
A defined evaluation yields a defined result.

⸻

II. Structural Logic Layer
	6.	Closure
All valid operations remain inside the defined system.
	7.	Traceability
Every state transition must be reconstructable.
	8.	Continuity
No silent resets or untracked mutations.
	9.	Constraint Precedence
Rules govern before interpretation.
	10.	Minimality
No primitive exists without necessity.
	11.	Composability
Valid components combine without contradiction.
	12.	Fixed-Point Stability
Re-evaluating a stable structure does not change it.

⸻

III. Execution Law
	13.	Deterministic Mapping
Identical inputs under identical rules produce identical outputs.
	14.	Scope Integrity
An object exists only within valid scope.
	15.	Boundary Finality
Statements terminate cleanly and completely.
	16.	Type Integrity
No implicit crossing of incompatible domains.
	17.	Guarded Access
No access to undefined state.
	18.	Order of Evaluation
Execution proceeds in defined sequence without retroactive mutation.

⸻

IV. Verification & Truth Discipline
	19.	Evidence Supremacy
Claims require demonstrable support.
	20.	Falsifiability
A proposition must be testable.
	21.	Reproducibility
Results must be independently replicable.
	22.	Measurability
Assertions must admit measurable criteria when applicable.
	23.	Error Exposure
Failure must be detectable, not suppressed.
	24.	No Silent Weakening
A constraint cannot degrade without explicit declaration.

⸻

V. Stability & Regeneration
	25.	Self-Containment
The system must survive without external dependency.
	26.	Regenerability
From any valid seed state, the structure can be reconstructed.
	27.	Entropy Containment
Drift must be bounded or corrected.
	28.	Invariant Preservation
Core axioms cannot be selectively bypassed.
	29.	Idempotent Revalidation
Rechecking integrity does not alter state.

⸻

VI. Governance Meta-Law
	30.	No Override Above Axiom
No layer supersedes the foundational substrate.
	31.	No Retroactive Repair of History
Past states are immutable records.
	32.	Single Reality Principle
Only one evaluated state may hold per context.
	33.	Silent Denial Rule
Invalid transitions do not execute.
	34.	Continuity Mandate
The system does not start or stop; it transitions.
